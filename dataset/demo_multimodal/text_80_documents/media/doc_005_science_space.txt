Executive Summary: Key takeaways are distilled below for rapid cross-disciplinary review.

A recent lunar crater ice core mission mapped crystalline water ice deposits preserved beneath shadowed polar craters. Mission engineers adjusted solar array pitch and thermal shields to extend orbital endurance. Spectral analysis confirmed repeatable absorption bands across infrared and ultraviolet channels.
